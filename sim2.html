ELA Teacher Cheat Sheet

Drop these 4 themes in class discussions or essays and your teacher will think you read every single page twice.

Theme #1

The Anthropocene (Humans Run the Planet)

"Anthropocene" is the current geological age where humans are the #1 force changing Earth. John shows how tiny things—like diet soda or lawn grass—have massive impacts on the planet.

💬 Say this in class: "Green shows that humans aren't just living on Earth—we are actively redesigning it."
Theme #2

Paying Attention = Caring

Reviewing things forces you to pay attention. Even when life feels overwhelming or depressing, noticing the weird beauty in everyday stuff (like a sunset or a video game) keeps you grounded.

💬 Say this in class: "To Green, paying close attention to ordinary objects is a form of love and hope."
Theme #3

The 5-Star Scale is Ridiculous (On Purpose)

You can't actually rate human pain or a childhood memory on a 1-to-5 star scale like an Amazon toaster. That's the point: real life is too messy and complex for simple internet ratings.

💬 Say this in class: "The 5-star format mocks how internet culture tries to reduce complex emotions into scores."
Theme #4

We Are Wired Together

Whether it's thousands of strangers cheering at a soccer match or scientists building the internet, humans survive because we connect and share experiences with each other.

💬 Say this in class: "Green argues that human connection is our species' greatest survival tool."
